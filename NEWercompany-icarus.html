DISCLOSURE REVIEW

Climate performance is easier to understand when the numbers tell the same story.

Icarus presents its climate information through emissions data, targets and operational indicators. The disclosure makes it possible to move from a headline sustainability claim to the underlying performance measures.

Illustrative case study. Icarus Wind & Solar is fictional. All figures, targets, scores and performance indicators on this page are illustrative and do not represent actual corporate disclosures.
