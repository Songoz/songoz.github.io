01 THE PURPOSE

Why this dashboard exists

Sustainability reports can stretch across more than a hundred pages, leaving important information buried among disclosures, calculations and supporting documentation.

For a public stakeholder, finding the figure that actually matters can become a project of its own.

This dashboard takes a different approach. It brings selected sustainability information into a visual environment where claims, figures and missing evidence can be examined together.

The Disclosure Lab presents four fictional companies with different approaches to sustainability reporting. Each case study focuses on a specific reporting area and examines how information is presented, what evidence is available, and where disclosure could be strengthened.
