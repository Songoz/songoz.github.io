02 / FLAGSHIP PROJECT

The Disclosure Lab.

Corporate sustainability reporting, performance indicators and the quality of disclosed information.

INTERACTIVE CASE STUDY

When sustainability becomes measurable.

Sustainability reports contain information about environmental, social and governance performance. But how clearly do they communicate progress?

The Disclosure Lab examines four fictional companies, each with a different reporting focus. The cases explore performance indicators, disclosure quality, evidence and areas for improvement.

All company profiles and figures in this lab are illustrative and simulated for analytical purposes.

03 / ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS

Reading the landscape.

GIS · SPATIAL ANALYSIS
ENVIRONMENTAL DATA

Environmental patterns have spatial consequences.

Environmental challenges are rarely distributed evenly. Geographic analysis can help reveal where exposure, access and resource pressures intersect.

This project explores the use of spatial data to investigate environmental conditions, identify patterns and communicate their implications.
