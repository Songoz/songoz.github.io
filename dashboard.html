A healthier planet means stronger businesses with better communication.

Sustainability information should not disappear inside a hundred-page report. It should be clear, accessible and meaningful to the people who rely on it.

This interactive dashboard brings ESG reporting to life through a collection of fictional company case studies. Each case study explores how sustainability information can be communicated effectively, or obscured through vague claims, selective figures and polished visual storytelling.

The dashboard moves beyond the traditional sustainability report to explore how clearer visual disclosure can make sustainability performance easier for public stakeholders to understand and question.
