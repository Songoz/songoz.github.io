SUSTAINABILITY REPORTING

A healthier planet means stronger businesses with better communication.

Sustainability reports can stretch across more than a hundred pages, leaving important information buried among technical disclosures, long explanations and extensive tables.

This interactive dashboard brings sustainability information to life through a collection of illustrative company case studies. It explores not only what organisations communicate about their environmental, social and governance performance, but also how the way that information is presented can affect what stakeholders actually understand.

The examples deliberately vary in reporting quality and communication style. Some make information easier to interpret, while others demonstrate how broad claims, attractive imagery and carefully selected figures can create an impression of progress without providing enough evidence to evaluate it.

The aim is simple: to make sustainability information easier for public stakeholders to find, understand and question.

WHY THIS DASHBOARD EXISTS

Sustainability information should not disappear inside the report.

A sustainability report can contain hundreds of pages of disclosures, calculations and supporting information. For a public stakeholder, finding the figure that actually matters can become a project of its own.

This dashboard takes a different approach. It puts selected sustainability information into a visual environment where claims, figures and missing evidence can be viewed together.
