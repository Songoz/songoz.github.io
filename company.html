LANGUAGE MATTERS

When the words are bigger than the evidence.

"Putting people at the heart of our transition."

Positive language without a clearly defined measure of what changed.

"Championing inclusion across our workforce."

The disclosure does not explain how inclusion is being measured.

"Creating meaningful community impact."

The number of people reached does not necessarily demonstrate the outcome created for those communities.

LOOK CLOSER

A sustainability claim is only as useful as the information surrounding it.

Transparent disclosure requires enough context for stakeholders to understand what was measured, how it was measured, what changed and where uncertainty remains.
