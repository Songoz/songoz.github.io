Galactic Energies

About the company

Galactic Energies is an illustrative energy company created for this portfolio case study. The example focuses on how environmental, social and governance information can be communicated, interpreted and questioned.

Its activities include energy generation, infrastructure development, employee programmes and community investment.

A fictional sustainability reporting example designed to examine the difference between persuasive communication and transparent disclosure.

TRANSPARENCY CHECK

What would a stakeholder still need to know?

The problem is not necessarily the claim itself. The problem is what the claim leaves unanswered.

01 Baseline

No previous-year figure is provided to establish what the 15% change is being measured against.

02 Definition

“Inclusive” is not clearly defined or linked to a measurable indicator.

03 Denominator

The disclosure does not explain whether the figure applies to all employees, leadership, new hires or another population.

04 Methodology

No explanation is provided for how the 15% figure was calculated.

05 Outcome

The existence of workshops does not demonstrate whether they changed employee or community outcomes.

06 Target

There is no clear future target against which stakeholders can assess progress.

A CLEARER DISCLOSURE

The same story can become much more useful when the evidence is visible.

This example provides a defined indicator, a reporting period, a baseline, a current figure and a future target. It gives a stakeholder something concrete to interpret rather than asking them to rely on a broad statement of progress.
