CASE STUDY 01 · CLIMATE & EMISSIONS

Icarus

Company evaluation

What Icarus does: Icarus Wind & Solar is a fictional renewable energy company operating wind and solar generation assets.

What stands out: Its climate disclosure connects emissions, targets and operational performance instead of presenting sustainability information as isolated statements.

2025 material topics: greenhouse gas emissions, renewable energy generation, purchased electricity, energy efficiency, operational fuel use and value-chain emissions.

A quarterly climate disclosure dashboard showing how a company can make environmental performance easier to interpret through transparent metrics, targets and progress tracking.

03 · DISCLOSURE QUALITY

What the disclosure makes visible

Reporting structure

Reporting boundary Operational assets and controlled activities are identified before emissions are reported.
Measurement Emissions are presented in tCO2e, while energy performance is reported using MWh and GJ.
Targets Actual performance is displayed alongside a defined target rather than as a standalone achievement.

Material climate topics · 2025

Greenhouse gases Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions are disclosed separately.
Energy Electricity and gas consumption are tracked quarterly to show operational movement.
Value chain Scope 3 is identified separately, making the company's indirect emissions visible.
